## Why Coaches Are Leaving ManyChat in 2026

If you are an online coach or course creator selling a high-ticket offer between $500 and $5,000, there is a good chance you have tried ManyChat at some point. For years it was the default choice for automating Instagram DMs, story replies, and comment-triggered sequences. But in 2026, the cracks are showing, and coaches are leaving in droves. Here is why. For a deeper look at how ManyChat compares specifically for Instagram DM automation and a step-by-step migration guide, see our [best ManyChat alternative for Instagram DM automation](/blog/best-manychat-alternative-instagram-dm-automation).

### The Per-Contact Pricing Trap

ManyChat's pricing model charges you based on the number of contacts in your database. That sounds reasonable until you realize what actually happens when you run a viral Reel or a giveaway campaign. A single piece of content can bring in 2,000 to 5,000 new contacts overnight. Suddenly your $15 per month plan jumps to $65, then $115, then higher. For coaches with 20K to 100K Instagram followers running regular content, monthly bills of $150 to $300 are common, and most of those contacts never convert into paying clients.

The math gets worse when you factor in that ManyChat counts every person who has ever messaged your page, not just active leads. You end up paying to store the contact details of people who commented "INFO" on a post nine months ago and never replied to your follow-up. Deleting contacts manually is tedious, and there is no automatic cleanup built into the platform.

### The Flow Builder Was Built for Marketers, Not Coaches

ManyChat's visual flow builder is powerful, but it was designed for e-commerce brands and digital marketers who think in terms of decision trees. As a coach, your sales process is fundamentally different. You are not trying to push someone down a funnel with yes-or-no buttons. You are trying to have a real conversation that uncovers pain points, builds trust, and invites someone to book a discovery call.

Building a ManyChat flow that feels conversational requires dozens of condition nodes, custom fields, delays, and randomized message variations. Most coaches spend hours building a flow only to end up with something that still feels robotic. The people in your DMs can tell when they are talking to a bot, and for high-ticket offers that depend on personal connection, that is a deal breaker.

### Messenger-First DNA

ManyChat was originally built for Facebook Messenger. Instagram DM automation was added later as a bolt-on feature, and it shows. The Instagram integration has historically lagged behind Messenger in terms of features, and certain capabilities like sending messages to people who have not interacted with your page first are restricted. If Instagram is your primary platform for generating leads and you are not running a Facebook-heavy strategy, you are using a tool that was not built with your workflow in mind.

Meta's ongoing API changes in 2025 and early 2026 have also introduced new compliance requirements that make keyword-triggered automations more fragile. Several coaches have reported flows breaking after API updates, with little warning from ManyChat's side.

All of this adds up to a simple reality: ManyChat is no longer the best fit for coaches who want to [how to automate your Instagram DMs](/blog/how-to-automate-instagram-dms-coaching-business) and convert followers into high-ticket clients. Fortunately, there are now purpose-built alternatives worth considering.

### 2. Inro --- Best for Advanced Segmentation

Inro has gained traction in the coaching space as a European-built Instagram automation tool with strong segmentation and CRM capabilities. It takes a data-first approach, letting you tag, segment, and score your Instagram audience based on their interactions, story views, and engagement patterns.

**Pros:**

- Audience scoring helps prioritize your warmest leads

**Cons:**

- Conversations still rely on pre-built flows, not AI, so responses can feel scripted
- The interface has a learning curve, especially for coaches who are not technically inclined
- Pricing scales with audience size, similar to ManyChat's model
- Limited integrations with coaching-specific tools like Calendly and ThriveCart

**Pricing:** Starts at around $39 per month for smaller audiences. Scales based on contact count.

**Best for:** Data-driven coaches who want deep audience insights and are comfortable building their own automation sequences.

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

**Pricing:** Starts at $29 per month. Simple flat-rate tiers.

**Best for:** Solo coaches and creators who publish content frequently and want basic DM follow-up without building complicated flows.

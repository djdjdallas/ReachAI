You have 12,000 Instagram followers. You have 8 paying coaching clients. Somewhere between those two numbers is the funnel that's either working or leaking.

Most coaches obsess over follower count. The coaches with full calendars don't. They obsess over the conversion path from follower to client and treat every stage like a system, not a vibe.

This guide breaks down the full path. Five stages, what coaches lose at each one, and the actual moves that turn an Instagram audience into paying clients.

## The 5-stage funnel

1. **Audience.** People who follow you and see your content.
2. **Engagement.** People who like, comment, or save.
3. **DM.** People who send you a direct message.
4. **Discovery call.** People who book a 25-30 minute call with you.
5. **Client.** People who pay for your coaching.

Most coaches focus on stages 1-2 (content) and stage 5 (the close). They underinvest in stages 3-4, which is where 80% of revenue is actually decided.

## Stage 1: Audience to Engagement

### What works

Posts that name a specific pain or outcome. {`"3 reasons your Instagram coaching DMs aren't converting"`} beats {`"some thoughts on coaching"`} every time.

The Reels that trigger the highest DM volume share three traits:
- Hook in the first 1.5 seconds
- One clear pain point per post (not three)
- Implicit CTA in the caption ({`"DM me 'X' for the breakdown"`})

### What to fix

If your engagement is high but DMs are low, your content is missing the implicit CTA. People are entertained but not invited to reach out. Add a closing line like {`"DM me 'method' if you want the full framework"`} or {`"comment 'workshop' below for the link"`} on your strongest posts.

## Stage 3: DM to Discovery Call

This is where the most money leaks in coaching businesses. Stage 3 is where qualifying conversations happen, and most coaches are bad at the qualifying conversation in DMs.

### What works

A 5-8 message exchange that qualifies on BTFU (Budget, Timeline, Fit, Urgency) before the booking link goes out. The order:

1. Acknowledge the lead's first message specifically
2. Ask what their goal is (Fit signal)
3. Ask what they've tried before (Fit + Budget signal)
4. Ask what triggered the reach-out (Urgency)
5. Ask about timeline (Timeline)
6. Surface budget signal indirectly ({`"have you considered investing in coaching to solve this?"`})
7. When 3 of 4 BTFU dimensions are positive, send the Calendly link with one sentence of context

### What to fix

The two most common stage-3 leaks:

**Replying too slowly.** Conversion drops 3x when a reply takes more than an hour and 5-10x when it takes more than a day. Manual replies can't keep up at scale.

**Sending the booking link too early.** Coaches who send Calendly in message 2 or 3 see no-show rates above 50%. Coaches who qualify first and send in message 7 see show rates above 80%.

## Stage 4: Discovery Call to Client

The discovery call is where the close happens. The single biggest variable here is who shows up. If your stage 3 (DM qualifying) was tight, the people who show up are pre-warmed, pre-qualified, and ready to make a real decision. If stage 3 was loose, you're running a 30-minute pitch with someone who isn't ready.

### What works

A discovery call agenda that mirrors the BTFU qualifying you did in DMs. First 5 minutes: confirm what they shared in DMs is still true. Next 10 minutes: go deeper on the goal and the blocker. Next 5 minutes: present your offer. Next 5 minutes: discuss investment. Last 5 minutes: clarify next steps.

### What to fix

If your show rate is below 70%, fix it before fixing anything else. Three moves:

1. **Pre-call DM the day before.** A warm message confirming the call and adding context.
2. **Reminders 24h and 1h before.** Email and SMS if possible.
3. **Reschedule link in every email.** Life happens. Don't make rescheduling friction.

If your close rate on discovery calls is below 25%, the leak is upstream. Better stage-3 qualification fixes stage-4 close rate more than any sales-call training can.

## Stage 5: Onboarding the new client

The post-purchase experience determines whether a client becomes a referral source. Three moves to install:

1. **Welcome DM within 5 minutes.** {`"Welcome aboard. I'm so glad you said yes. Watch for an email in the next hour with everything you need."`}
2. **First 48 hours over-deliver.** Send your onboarding materials, schedule the first session, give them a quick win they can hold onto.
3. **Ask for a testimonial at the right moment.** The right moment is week 2-3, when the early excitement has translated into a tangible result. Capture it in their voice.

A coach with strong stage 5 generates 30-50% of new clients from referrals. A coach with weak stage 5 spends every dollar on cold acquisition.
